WHY COVERAGE TWIN EXISTS

Coverage decisions are rarely blocked by a lack of data. They are blocked because the data arrives in different formats, at different times, across different systems.

Policy clauses, hospital evidence, clinical facts, cost estimates and human judgement all need to agree before a consequential decision can be trusted.

Coverage Twin keeps those pieces close, structured and traceable from first intake to final resolution.

AI is good at analysis.
Humans own the consequential call.

HUMAN GOVERNANCE

The system can classify, cross-check, retrieve evidence and produce a recommendation at machine speed.

The final call still needs an accountable reviewer whenever the case is consequential, ambiguous or exceptional.

BRILLIANT AT

Finding the relevant evidence, comparing facts and preserving consistency.

NEVER ALLOWED TO HIDE

Uncertainty, contradictions, missing evidence or a decision that requires human judgement.

The machine narrows the problem. The reviewer owns the decision.
